# Assignee Filter for Linear: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Assignee Filter for Linear does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

**What it reads.** To show its avatar bar, the extension reads team member names, avatar links, team memberships and workspace details from the copy of your data that Linear already keeps in your browser. This information is only used to display the bar on the Linear page you're viewing. The extension doesn't save it anywhere.

**What it stores.** One setting: whether the "Show assignee filter" switch is on. It stays in your browser's extension storage.

**What it changes.** When you click an avatar, the extension updates the filter in the page's URL. Linear then applies that filter, as it does with its own filter controls. The extension doesn't change your issues.

**Network requests.** The extension has no servers and sends no analytics. The only thing it loads is team members' avatar images, from the links Linear provides.

**Other sites.** The extension only runs on https://linear.app.

This extension is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Linear.
